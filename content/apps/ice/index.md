Ice hides menu bar items you do not need all the time and shows them again when you ask. Items go into a visible section, a hidden section or an always-hidden section, and you arrange them by dragging in Ice's settings. Hidden items come back when you hover over the menu bar, click an empty part of it, scroll or swipe on it, or press a hotkey. They can also hide again on their own.

On a MacBook with a notch, the Ice Bar shows hidden items in a separate strip below the menu bar, so they no longer disappear behind the notch. Other features include:

- a search panel for finding a menu bar item;
- hiding an app's menus when they would overlap visible items;
- a solid or gradient tint, shadow, border, and rounded or split shape for the bar itself;
- adjustable spacing between items, marked as beta.

Ice covers the main job Bartender does, hiding and rearranging menu bar items, and costs nothing; the developer takes donations. Layout profiles, item groups and items that appear under trigger conditions are on its roadmap but not built yet.

Ice needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later because it depends on system APIs added in that version. The download is a universal zip for Apple silicon and Intel, signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, and the app can update itself. Its last stable release came out in October 2024. Builds that fix problems on macOS 26 Tahoe have so far been published only as pre-releases, the newest in September 2025.
