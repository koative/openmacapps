Textual connects you to IRC networks and keeps your servers, channels and private messages in one Mac window, in a light or dark appearance with matching chat styles. Typing gets nickname and channel completion and rich text formatting. Inline images, notifications and a quick channel search cover the rest of the daily chat.

- Connections support IRCv3, IPv6, SASL and client certificate login, plus ZNC and other bouncers.
- SSL/TLS settings, certificate trust and proxies can be configured, and OTR can encrypt some private conversations.
- Rule-based ignore lists, message filters and an address book help keep busy channels readable.
- Chat styles are written in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so you can restyle the conversation view.
- Scripts and addons extend the app in AppleScript, Swift, Python and other languages.

Textual began in 2010 as a fork of LimeChat, which is why part of the code carries LimeChat's two-clause BSD license. Blendbyte took over maintenance from Codeux Software in October 2026 and shipped Textual 7.2.8 later that month. Code for the shipping version 7 sits on the `v7` branch, while `main` is being rebuilt for Textual 8.

The download is a notarized DMG for macOS 12 Monterey or later. It runs free for 30 days, after which it needs a licence, which covers one person on the Macs they own. Once activated, the licence is checked by the app itself without contacting a server. Textual is no longer sold on the Mac App Store.
