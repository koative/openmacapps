Disk X-Ray is inspired by WinDirStat (https://github.com/windirstat/windirstat,
GPL-3.0-or-later): the idea of a file tree, a file-type list and a treemap kept
in sync, and its scanning strategy (bulk directory reads with parallel
workers), which MAC-LIMPO implements on macOS with getattrlistbulk(2).

The treemap uses the squarified layout described by M. Bruls, K. Huizing and
J. J. van Wijk, "Squarified Treemaps" (Data Visualization 2000).
