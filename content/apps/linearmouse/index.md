LinearMouse changes how a mouse or trackpad moves the pointer and scrolls, with separate settings for each device. A common use is keeping natural scrolling on the built-in trackpad while reversing it on an external mouse.

- Scrolling can be reversed per device and adjusted in each direction.
- Pointer speed and acceleration can be tuned, reset to the system defaults or switched off entirely.
- Buttons and scroll actions can be remapped to keyboard shortcuts, media controls, Mission Control and gestures.
- Profiles apply settings by device, by app and by display.

The download is a DMG with a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, signed with a Developer ID and notarized; version 0.12.0 sets macOS 10.15 as its minimum. It is also available as the `linearmouse` Homebrew cask. Translations come from contributors, through pull requests or Crowdin.
