QCAD Community Edition draws 2D plans and technical drawings in DXF files. You place lines, arcs, circles, ellipses, splines and polylines with snapping to endpoints, centres, intersections, tangents or typed coordinates, then annotate them with text, hatches and dimensions.

- Layers and blocks organise a drawing, with nested blocks and dockable lists for both.
- Dimensions cover aligned, linear, radius, diameter and angle measurements, plus leaders.
- Printing works to a fixed scale such as 1:100, with a print preview, and drawings export to PDF and bitmap images.
- An ECMAScript interface creates and changes entities and can add new interactive tools.

The macOS download is a trial of QCAD Professional. It runs for 15 minutes at a time and can be restarted. The commercial add-ons can be removed to leave the GPL Community Edition, which keeps only the basic CAD features. DWG files, hierarchical layers, external references, polyline offsets and poster printing belong to the paid editions. The Community Edition saves DXF R15 (2000).

It is listed here as an alternative to AutoCAD for 2D drafting with layers, blocks and dimensions. The QCAD source code is GPLv3, and builds from it need Qt 5 or Qt 6.

QCAD 27.0.3 comes as separate DMGs for Apple silicon and Intel, built for macOS 12.0 or later, with other packages for macOS 14.4 and later. The Apple silicon DMG is signed with RibbonSoft's Developer ID and notarized.
