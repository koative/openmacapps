OpenTTD has you run a transport company. You lay rail, roads, canals and airports between towns and industries, then buy trains, buses, ships and aircraft and give them orders to carry passengers and cargo.

It is based on Transport Tycoon Deluxe by Chris Sawyer and stays close to it, with many additions:

- maps up to 64 times the original size
- multiplayer with as many as 255 players sharing 15 companies, and dedicated servers
- path-based signals, presignals and conditional orders
- autoreplace and cloning for vehicles, and stations you can join together
- AI opponents written by players

The game needs graphics and sound sets. The free OpenGFX graphics, OpenSFX sounds and OpenMSX music fill that gap, or you can copy the data files from a Transport Tycoon Deluxe CD. AIs, NewGRF add-ons, scenarios and heightmaps download inside the game through Check Online Content. Every version loads saved games from every older one.

Version 15.3 for the Mac is a universal app, offered as a disk image or a ZIP. The download page lists macOS 10.15 for Intel and macOS 11 for Apple silicon. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized. OpenTTD is also free on Steam and GOG.
