Macboard is a small clipboard manager for macOS. It keeps a searchable list of copied text and images, shows link previews, highlights hyperlinks, and lets you pin items you reuse. Everything is driven from the keyboard: ⇧⌘V opens the list, and the shortcuts for pasting, pinning and deleting can be customised. It suits people who want a clipboard history that stays out of the way.

It works on macOS 12 Monterey or later. The DMG is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so the first launch has to be allowed in System Settings under Privacy & Security. The README says the project is not notarized because a Developer ID certificate was too costly for a free project. The last release, v1.6, dates from January 2025.
