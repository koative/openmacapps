Xournal++ gives you pages to write and draw on with a pen tablet or mouse, and it can load a PDF as the background so you annotate on top of it. Pressure-sensitive styluses from makers such as Wacom, Huion and XP-Pen work, and stylus or mouse buttons can be mapped to different tools and colours.

On a PDF background you can select its text to copy, highlight, underline or strike it through, and follow its links. Blank pages take paper backgrounds for notes, scratch work or a whiteboard. The finished file exports to PDF, PNG or SVG from the window or from the command line.

- Pen, highlighter and eraser tools, with solid, dotted and other stroke styles
- Lines, arrows, circles, rectangles and splines, plus set-square and compass guides
- Layers you can hide or edit one at a time, and a sidebar of page previews for reordering pages
- A LaTeX editor for equations and formulas, which needs a LaTeX installation on the Mac
- Audio recorded while you write, attached to objects in the note and played back with the Play Object tool
- Plugins written in Lua

Xournal++ rewrites the older Xournal program and reuses parts of its code, including the stroke recognizer. It also runs on Linux and Windows. PDF-XChange Editor, by contrast, lists Windows 10 and later as its only supported systems, so on a Mac you need something else to write, draw and highlight over PDF pages.

The 1.3.8 release has separate disk images for Apple silicon and Intel, each requiring macOS 10.15 or later. Both apps carry only an ad hoc signature, and the README says macOS refuses to run them. It gives two Terminal commands that strip the app's extended attributes and re-sign it locally.
