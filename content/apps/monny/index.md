Monny shows CPU, memory, disk, network, core and battery readings in a menu bar popover, with a dashboard window for history charts, top processes and temperature sensors. Its battery control sets the macOS charge limit between 80 and 100 percent, so macOS keeps enforcing it after Monny quits. Readings stay on the Mac, and the app has no account, analytics or telemetry. It suits people who want a compact status display and a way to limit battery wear.

It requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 15 or later; Intel Macs are not supported. The charge limit needs a Mac with a battery and macOS 26.4 or later. The build is signed with an Apple Development certificate and is not notarized, so the first launch has to be allowed under System Settings > Privacy & Security with Open Anyway. The optional AI usage limits are off by default and contact the providers you enable.
