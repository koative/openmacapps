Folo pulls blogs, news sites, newsletters, podcasts, videos and social posts into one timeline that you read on the Mac, the web or your phone. Subscriptions, read status and stars stay in sync across every place you use it.

- Any RSS, Atom or JSON feed works, and RSSHub routes cover sites that publish no feed. OPML import and export moves your list in or out.
- Newsletters can go to an inbox with its own email address.
- Articles, pictures, videos and podcasts get separate layouts, and text-to-speech can read an article aloud.
- Actions apply your own rules to new entries, for example to star, filter or summarise them.
- Items go out to Obsidian, Readwise, Instapaper, Zotero and several other services.

You can ask Folo AI about one article, a folder or everything you follow, get a digest of a busy timeline, or read a translation beside the original in English, Chinese, Japanese or French. The model is your choice, and your own API key works too. Scheduled tasks can send daily or weekly briefings to chat or email.

Reeder also gathers feeds, videos and social posts in one timeline, but it is an App Store app for iOS and macOS that syncs through iCloud. Folo adds Windows, Linux, Android and a browser version, plus the AI tools.

The desktop app comes as separate Apple silicon and Intel downloads, and the Mac App Store carries it too. The Apple silicon build from the 1.17.0 release requires macOS 13 and is notarized. The code is AGPL-3.0, except the bundled MingCute icons in `icons/mgc`, which may not be redistributed.
