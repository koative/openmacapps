mFONTS keeps your fonts in a catalogue where you browse, sort and activate them without installing every file. Fonts are referenced where they already sit, never copied. You group them into libraries and collections, tag them, mark favorites and filter by status, properties or language, and a filtered view can be saved as a collection. Smart lists show active, inactive and auto-activated fonts, and another list shows duplicates.

Previews render the real fonts in a grid or a list, with your own sample text and size. Activation goes through Core Text, either for the current session or persistently, and works per family or in batches.

Activation can also follow your documents. Open a file through mFONTS and it activates the fonts the document asks for, then opens it in its usual app. It can also watch documents opened in apps such as Pages, Photoshop, InDesign, Word and DaVinci Resolve. It reads font names from formats including PSD, PDF, IDML, DOCX and Pages. Extensions for Photoshop and InDesign can be installed from the app.

A Find fonts drawer searches the free catalogues of Fontsource, which includes Google Fonts, plus Fontshare and Font Squirrel. A family you pick is downloaded into a Downloaded library and activated. Logins for other font sites are kept in the macOS Keychain.

Existing Right Fonts users can import its libraries, collections, tags and preferences read-only; Right Fonts itself is left untouched. mFONTS is not affiliated with it. The app can run at login as a menu bar service, its interface is in French, English, Spanish and Italian, and it updates from GitHub releases. The release is built for Apple silicon, requires macOS 15, and is Developer ID signed and notarized.
