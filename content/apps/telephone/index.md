Telephone makes and takes phone calls on the Mac through a SIP account. You add the details from a VoIP provider or your company's phone server, and the Mac replaces a desk phone wherever it has a decent network connection. Without such an account it cannot place calls.

- Several SIP accounts can be set up at once.
- Contact names autocomplete, and clicking a `sip:` or `tel:` link starts a call.
- Calls can be muted, held and transferred, with call waiting and a call history.
- Tone signals are sent by pressing number keys while the call window is active.
- TLS and SRTP encryption are supported.

The app is free on the Mac App Store from its developer, 64 Characters, and needs macOS 10.13 or later. A Telephone Pro subscription unlocks the full call history and up to 30 simultaneous calls, and the developer asks for it to fund continued work.

The source is published under the GPL, but pull requests are not accepted for legal reasons; ideas go into the issue tracker. The latest App Store version, 1.6, dates from June 2022, and the repository has received commits through 2026.
