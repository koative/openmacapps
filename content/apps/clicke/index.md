Clické captures an area, a window or the full screen from the menu bar or with global shortcuts, then shows a floating preview. Its editor adds backgrounds, window mockups, shadows, rounded corners, layered screenshots and canvas sizes for common social networks, and exports PNG or JPEG. Its redaction tools pixelate, blur or cover areas, and a Find Sensitive Text action spots email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers and API keys. It suits people who share screenshots often and want them tidy and free of private details.

It requires macOS 26.1 or later and the Screen Recording permission, which the app asks for on the first capture. The README says the app makes no network connections and that text recognition runs on the Mac; global shortcuts do not need Accessibility access. The README does not state that the build is notarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, use Open Anyway in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
