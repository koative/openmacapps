Flycast plays Sega Dreamcast games, along with arcade titles built for Sega's Naomi and Naomi 2 boards and the Atomiswave system. It grew out of the reicast emulator and runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, Android, Nintendo Switch and Xbox.

Releases still bring support for individual games. Version 2.7, from August 2026, fixed online features in titles such as F355 Challenge and Cyber Troopers Virtual-On: Oratorio Tangram, and added multiscreen output for three games: Derby Owners Club, Airline Pilot and Sega Strike Fighter. The same release added frame rate detection and pacing, improved modem emulation, and translations into French, Hungarian, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese and Swedish. It also works better with DreamPicoPort and DreamPotato.

On a Mac you choose between three channels. Tagged stable releases come as a ZIP on GitHub. The project's Homebrew tap installs stable, master or nightly builds, and the README recommends master. Nightly development builds are experimental. The 2.7 macOS ZIP holds a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel that runs on macOS 10.15 or later.

Settings and supported features are documented in a community wiki that the README links to. Flycast is licensed under GPL-2.0.
