Readest opens your ebooks and documents in one reader and keeps them in a library you can sort and organize. You drop in EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, CBZ, TXT, HTML or Markdown files, then read them in scrolling or page-by-page mode.

- Highlights, bookmarks and notes stay attached to each book.
- Full-text search covers one book or the current library shelf.
- Words can be looked up in built-in dictionaries and Wikipedia, or in local dictionary packs you import.
- Parallel Read places two books or documents side by side, handy for a text and its translation.
- Text-to-speech reads aloud, and EPUB 3 media overlays play with the matching text highlighted.
- OPDS catalogs and Calibre libraries can be browsed from the app.

Font, layout and theme colors are adjustable. The app also offers full keyboard navigation and works with VoiceOver.

Readest runs on Windows, Linux, iOS, Android and the web as well, and it can sync books, reading progress, notes and bookmarks between them. Sync uses a Readest account, and the free plan includes it along with a small cloud storage quota and daily translation allowance; paid plans raise those limits. Progress and notes can also sync with KOReader devices.

The Mac version comes from the App Store or as a Universal DMG on GitHub for Apple silicon and Intel, requiring macOS 12 or later.
