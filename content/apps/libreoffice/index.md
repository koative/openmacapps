LibreOffice handles writing documents, building spreadsheets and preparing slide decks on local files. It installs as one app with six modules: Writer for text, Calc for spreadsheets, Impress for presentations, Draw for diagrams and page layout, Base for databases, and Math for formulas.

Its native format is OpenDocument (.odt, .ods, .odp), and it opens and saves Microsoft Office files such as .docx, .xlsx and .pptx. Writer, Calc and Impress cover the same everyday jobs as Word, Excel and PowerPoint. Unlike Microsoft 365, it needs no account or subscription and works without a cloud service.

The project's system requirements page lists macOS 11 or newer on an Intel or Apple silicon processor. The download page offers separate disk images for Apple silicon and Intel rather than one universal app. A few optional features rely on Java, and on macOS the project says they need a full JDK rather than a JRE.
