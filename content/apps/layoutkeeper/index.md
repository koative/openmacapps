LayoutKeeper is a menu bar app for multi-monitor Mac setups. It remembers where windows sat on each display and Space, then puts them back when you reconnect a monitor or wake from sleep.

It can also sync external display brightness with the built-in ambient light sensor, switch monitor arrangement presets by USB-C port, and offer four levels of sleep prevention. Requires macOS 26 or later.
