MuseScore Studio is where you write out sheet music: enter notes onto the page, lay out the score, and play it back to hear what you wrote. The page you edit is the page that prints, and you can export it as a PDF.

Note entry is built to be quick, and a connected MIDI keyboard can enter notes too. The handbook covers the details of writing for guitar, harp, percussion and keyboard alongside the usual pitch, rhythm, repeats and text.

- MusicXML, MIDI and MEI files import and export, and MuseData files import, so scores move between MuseScore and other notation programs.
- A built-in synthesizer plays the score, with a mixer for each instrument and support for SoundFonts.
- VST instruments and effects already installed on the Mac show up in the mixer.
- Muse Sounds libraries install through the separate Muse Hub app, which lives in the menu bar.

The Mac download page offers two versions: one bundled with Muse Hub, which needs macOS 12 or higher, and MuseScore Studio on its own. Finished scores can be uploaded to musescore.com or shared on Audio.com from inside the app, though nothing requires it.

The desktop program is free and open source under GPL version 3.
