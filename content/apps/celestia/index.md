Celestia is a 3D space simulator: instead of showing the sky from one spot on Earth, it lets you fly anywhere in a modelled universe and look back. It opens on Earth against a starfield. Click any object to select it, press G to travel there, and right-drag to orbit it. The mouse wheel changes your distance, and the zoom spans scales from galaxy clusters down to spacecraft a few metres across.

- A Tour Guide in the Navigation menu lists objects worth visiting and takes you to them.
- The Star Browser lists the 100 nearest stars, or the brightest ones or those with planets, as seen from wherever you are.
- The Solar System Browser shows the objects of the nearest solar system as a tree, if one lies within a light year of you.
- You can find stars by common name, Bayer designation, or HIP, HD and SAO catalogue number.

You can also move through time as well as space, and the screen shows the simulation's current time in UT. The built-in catalogue covers stars and galaxies, planets and their moons, asteroids, comets and spacecraft. You can add real or invented objects of your own by writing plain-text catalogue files for solar systems, stars or deep-sky objects and dropping them into the extras folders, or install add-ons that others have published.

On the Mac, the project's download page points to the Mac App Store for version 1.7.0. The GitHub release is version 1.6.4 from November 2023, a ZIP with a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel that is Developer ID-signed and notarized, and that is the build Homebrew installs. Celestia also runs on Windows, Linux, iOS and Android.
