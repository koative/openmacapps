Wallnetic plays a video file as your desktop background. It carries no large catalogue of its own: you drag in an MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM or GIF, or open its built-in browser to search sites such as Pexels, Pixabay and Wallhaven and pull clips into the library.

- Each display can play a different video, or all of them can share one.
- Trim loops a chosen part of a clip without changing or re-encoding the original.
- Brightness, blur, tint and other effects come with eight presets, and an optional clock and date can sit on the wallpaper.
- A daily schedule and playlists rotate wallpapers, and on macOS 14 or later Shortcuts actions can switch or pause them.

Decoding pauses on a locked Mac, a sleeping display or Low Power Mode, and by default whenever a fullscreen app has the front. Normal windows stacked over the desktop leave the video running. There is no live playback on the lock screen; Wallnetic can instead set a still frame from the current video as the system wallpaper.

The developer says the app needs no account, has no subscription or paid tier and collects no analytics. It runs on macOS 13 or later and is also on the Mac App Store. The GitHub release has separate disk images for Apple silicon and Intel, ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS refuses the first launch until you allow it in Privacy & Security.

Wallpaper Engine is a Windows app whose editor turns still images into animated scenes, and its Steam Workshop holds hundreds of thousands of shared wallpapers. Wallnetic cannot render those scenes. It plays ordinary video, so a Workshop item works only when it is a plain video file saved on your Mac.
