Simplenote stores your notes in a Simplenote account and syncs each change, without a sync button, to its apps for iOS, Android, Windows and Linux and to the browser. You find notes with instant search and organize them with tags and pins.

- Checklists go into a note from the editor toolbar.
- Markdown notes can be written, previewed and published.
- Every change is backed up, so you can return to what a note said last week or last month.
- You can share a note with other people or publish it on the web.
- Notes show up in Spotlight, and several can be open in separate windows.

You need a Simplenote account to use the app, and version 2.21 added sign-in by magic link. You can also delete the account from inside the app. The apps, sync, backups and sharing are free.

Google Keep also syncs notes and lists across devices in real time, and its notes can hold photos, drawings and audio and carry reminders. Simplenote's Mac client is open source under the GPL 2.0 and syncs through the Simperium protocol.

The Mac app comes from the Mac App Store and needs macOS 12 or later. Version 2.21, from September 2024, is the current release there, and GitHub has no newer version tag, while commits to the repository continue.
