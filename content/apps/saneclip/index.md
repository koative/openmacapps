SaneClip keeps a history of the text and images you copy and brings any of them back from the keyboard. Press Control-Shift-Command-Y to open the history, or Control-Command and a digit from 1 to 9 to paste one of the latest items directly.

Protection is the focus. You can exclude apps such as 1Password from capture entirely, and the app flags copied credit card numbers, API keys, passwords and private keys. Flagged items can be set to delete themselves after a minute, five minutes or an hour. History can be encrypted on disk with AES-256-GCM and locked behind Touch ID. The developer says clipboard contents never reach its servers.

- Snippets fill in placeholders for the date, the time or a value you type when pasting.
- Paste As converts text to upper or lower case, pretty-prints JSON or strips HTML.
- Clipboard rules remove tracking parameters from URLs and trim whitespace on every copy.
- A saneclip:// URL scheme and Shortcuts actions handle automation.

SaneClip requires macOS 14 or later and Apple silicon. The download from saneclip.com is notarized and opens with a 14-day trial of every feature; after that, the paid Pro unlock is required to keep using it. Its source is published under PolyForm Shield 1.0.0, which forbids using it for a competing product.

Paste also keeps a clipboard history that stays in sync across Mac, iPhone and iPad. SaneClip keeps its history on the Mac by default, and syncing to its free iPhone and iPad companion is optional and runs through your own iCloud account.
