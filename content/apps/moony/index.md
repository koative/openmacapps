Moony puts bank accounts, stocks, crypto, bonds, real estate, loans and insurance into one net-worth view, with statement import, budgets, cashflow reports and spending plans. Records, photos and documents are stored AES-256 encrypted on your computer, and there is no account, cloud or sync. The README says the app contacts the internet only for market data and update checks, which PRIVACY.md lists. It suits people with several accounts and investments who do not want to give their statements to a service. It is not available on phones or tablets, and it is not built for shared household budgets.

The README lists macOS builds for Apple silicon and Intel but does not state a minimum macOS version. It says the Mac app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Moony is free software under AGPL-3.0, and the README offers a commercial license on request. It is not financial, tax or investment advice, and Yahoo Finance prices can be rate-limited, so a price can be set by hand.
