Table Habit tracks micro habits, the small things you check in each day. Each habit gets a score for how consistent you have been, beyond a count of check-ins, and growth curves chart that score so you can see the long-term trend.

- Habits can be ones you want to do or ones you want to stop, and each kind is scored by its own model.
- Every habit can have its own color, and habits can be grouped, collapsed and reordered by dragging.
- Search and filters find old habits quickly.
- Data exports and imports as readable JSON, and you can bring over a history from Loop Habit Tracker.

Everything works offline with no account. To use the same habits on a phone, you point each copy at a WebDAV server such as Nextcloud, Koofr or one you host yourself. The developer says the app has no ads, in-app purchases or telemetry.

The Flutter app also runs on iPhone, Android, Windows and Linux. On the Mac it comes from the Mac App Store, the developer's Homebrew tap, or as a disk image on GitHub holding a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel that needs macOS 12 or later.
