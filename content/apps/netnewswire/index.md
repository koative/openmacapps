NetNewsWire collects new articles from the blogs and news sites you subscribe to and keeps track of what you have read. A Safari extension helps you add feeds, and NetNewsWire refreshes it in the background.

It reads RSS, Atom, JSON Feed and RSS-in-JSON. Subscriptions can sync through iCloud or a sync service: Feedly, Feedbin, Inoreader, FreshRSS, NewsBlur, BazQux or The Old Reader, and you can run several accounts side by side.

- Keyboard navigation works with single-key shortcuts.
- Smart feeds gather All Unread and Today, and read articles and feeds can be hidden.
- A reader view and customizable article themes change how posts look.
- Articles share to Mail, Notes, Messages, MarsEdit, Micro.blog and other apps.
- Feed lists import and export as OPML, and AppleScript can drive the Mac app.

Reeder syncs its subscriptions and reading position through iCloud. NetNewsWire offers iCloud too, plus the hosted services above, and the same app runs on iPhone and iPad.

The Mac app needs macOS 15 or later. It comes as a ZIP with a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, signed with a Developer ID and notarized. It is free under the MIT license, and the project asks for no money.
