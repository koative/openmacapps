OBS Studio records and streams whatever you arrange in a scene. You stack sources such as a window capture, a webcam, a capture card, an image, text or a browser window, then record the result to a file or broadcast it live.

- You can keep as many scenes as you like and switch between them with transitions, including your own stinger video.
- The audio mixer gives each source its own filters, for example gain, a noise gate or noise suppression, and accepts VST plugins.
- Hotkeys cover nearly every action: changing scenes, starting or stopping a stream or recording, muting a source and push to talk.
- Studio Mode shows the next scene in a preview so you can adjust it before viewers see it.
- The interface is built from docks that you can rearrange or pop out into separate windows.

Multiview puts eight scenes on screen at once, so you can watch them all and cue or cut to one with a click or a double click. When the built-in features run out, native plugins and Lua or Python scripts can work with your existing sources.

The Mac download comes as two disk images, one for Apple silicon and one for Intel. Version 33.0.1 needs macOS 13 or later, and the app is notarized by Apple. OBS Studio is free, licensed under the GPL version 2 or any later version.
