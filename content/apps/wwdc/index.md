WWDC puts Apple's developer conference on your Mac: the keynote and the Platforms State of the Union as live streams, every session video as it comes out during the week, and the archive from earlier years. You browse, watch and mark what you have seen, then come back to the sessions you saved for later.

- Explore lists new content, unfinished videos, recent favorites and anything streaming live.
- Schedule shows each day of the current conference and plays its live streams.
- Transcripts are searchable, come in several languages, and jump the video to the matching line.
- Bookmarks mark a moment in a video with a note, and search covers those notes too.
- Playback runs from 0.5x to 2x or a custom speed, in full screen or picture in picture.

Clip Sharing exports a segment of up to five minutes from a session. Shared session links point to Apple's developer website and open in the app on a Mac that has it installed.

With iCloud sync turned on, favorites, bookmarks and viewing progress follow you across your Macs. Builds you compile yourself have CloudKit features switched off.

The app is an independent project and not made by Apple. Version 7.5 from June 2024 is the latest release, while the repository still gets commits. It requires macOS 12 or later and ships as a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, notarized.
