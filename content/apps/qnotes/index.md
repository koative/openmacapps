qNOTES keeps a set of rich-text notes that you write with headings, styles, colors and alignment. Bulleted, numbered and task lists are supported, along with links, and smart bullets continue a list as you type.

Each note is saved as its own RTF file with a plain-text copy beside it, so the notes stay readable outside the app. You order them freely, add custom separators and watch a word and character counter.

Quick access comes from two places. A panel opens from the menu bar or with ⌃⌥N, and a desktop widget in small, medium or large size shows recent or pinned notes. Daily backups run on their own and can be restored from the app, and sync between Macs goes through iCloud Drive when you turn it on.

Notes can be imported from Tot backups or from JSON, Markdown and text files. The app takes its idea from Tot and is not affiliated with its authors. Releases are arm64 builds for macOS 14 or later, notarized, with the interface in English, French, Spanish and Italian and a daily update check.
