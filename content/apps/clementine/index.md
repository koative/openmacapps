Clementine plays your music library and organizes it, from tags and cover art to playlists in tabs. Point it at your music folders, search the library, and drag songs into a playlist.

Beyond local files it reaches many outside sources. It streams internet radio from SomaFM, Digitally Imported, Radio-Browser.info, Icecast and others, and plays music from Jamendo, Magnatune, Subsonic and Plex servers. It also finds songs you uploaded to Box, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive or Seafile.

- Smart and dynamic playlists, with import and export of M3U, XSPF, PLS, ASX and WPL files and support for CUE sheets.
- Tag editing, missing tags from MusicBrainz, and missing cover art from Last.fm, MusicBrainz or Discogs.
- Transcoding to MP3, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, FLAC, AAC, WAV and other formats.
- Podcasts through gpodder.net, an equalizer, projectM visualizations and lyrics shown in time with the song when they are timed.

Clementine is inspired by Amarok 1.4. It runs on Windows and Linux too, and a new numbered release comes out in any week with changes users will notice. The 1.4.4 Mac DMG is for Apple silicon only and needs macOS 26 or later. The app inside is Developer ID-signed and notarized; the DMG itself is not signed.

It scrobbles to Last.fm. You can copy music to an iPod, MTP device or USB player. It can play on a Chromecast, and an Android app, an iPhone or iPad, or the command line can control it.
