Unfoldly indexes the folders you choose (documents, spreadsheets, slides, images, audio, video and notes) and lets you search them by description rather than by file name. You can ask follow-up questions about a file it finds, such as summarising a document or asking about a recording. It is aimed at people with large, messy folders who remember what a file was about but not what it was called.

The README describes the current public build as a signed, notarized macOS app. It does not state a minimum macOS version, and it describes the app as early beta. Indexes, downloaded models, chat history and logs stay in the app's local data folder; the app may still go online to download models or releases. Uninstalling removes indexes, models and settings through a script in the repository, and leaves your original files in place.
