ClipPocket saves everything you copy and lists it under a clipboard icon in the menu bar, or in a panel you open with Command-Shift-V. Click an item to copy it back, or turn on auto-paste to have it pasted as soon as you select it.

- It recognises URLs, colours, code, files and images, and you can filter by type or search the text.
- Right-clicking an item pins it to keep it at hand.
- Pushing the pointer to the bottom edge of the screen can show the panel, with show and hide delays of up to two seconds.
- File references copied in Finder can be pasted again from the history.
- A snippet manager, added in version 2.6, stores reusable text by category, with {placeholder} fields for templates.

Incognito mode pauses recording, and settings can clear the history. Since 2.6 the history on disk can be encrypted with AES-256. The developer says all data stays on your Mac, with no cloud sync or outside servers.

The app in the 2.7 download, which reports itself as version 2.6.0, needs macOS 13 or later and runs on Apple silicon or Intel. It checks for its own updates. The app has only an ad hoc signature, so Gatekeeper rejects it until you allow it in System Settings.

Paste keeps its history on Mac, iPhone and iPad. ClipPocket stays on one Mac.
