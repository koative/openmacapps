LaunchNow puts your apps back in a paged grid of icons. It runs in the background and opens either as a classic full-screen Launchpad or in a window style, and you can switch between the two.

- Dragging one app onto another makes a folder, and apps move in and out of folders freely.
- Holding Option locks the other icons in place while you build a folder.
- Search covers every app, including those inside folders.
- Hidden apps leave the grid without being deleted.

Tab and Shift-Tab flip pages, the arrow keys select an app and Return launches it. A four-finger trackpad gesture opens the grid, and you can configure it. You can also set the icon size, the scroll sensitivity for turning pages and whether the frosted glass background is on. The interface comes in 13 languages and switches without a restart.

Layouts export to a file and import again, which doubles as a backup. To have LaunchNow ready after startup, add it to Login Items in System Settings.

Version 1.9.2 requires macOS 26 and comes as a ZIP holding a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel. LaunchNext, another Launchpad replacement, credits LaunchNow as the project it is built upon.
