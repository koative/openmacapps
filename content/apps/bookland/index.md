Hover beside the MacBook camera to open Bookland's panel, then choose a workspace for music, events, files or coding activity. Move the pointer away and the panel closes; a click can open it too. The compact strip stays near the notch while you work in other apps.

The Music workspace reads the current macOS media session and presents playback controls and a seek bar. Browser players can work through that session, although individual controls depend on the player. The Calendar workspace shows a month grid with event markers and upcoming events from calendars already connected to macOS. Calendar access needs a separate macOS permission.

Bookland also keeps a recent-file tray and a Pocket for dropping files. Its Codex workspace brings activity and answers into the island, so you can check progress without opening the main Codex window. These workspaces share the same small panel rather than taking over the screen. Settings let you change the hover close delay and choose which tools appear.

The current download is a free 0.1.4 preview for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later. It has no Bookland account or subscription. The ZIP is locally signed but not Apple-notarized, so the first launch may need macOS's Open Anyway flow. Media support and permission-dependent features can vary by Mac and player. The project has a public issue tracker and a first-look discussion for test reports.
