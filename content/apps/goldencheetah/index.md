GoldenCheetah takes the files from your bike computer, power meter or training service and turns them into charts and models of your training. It was built for cyclists, triathletes and their coaches. You can plan sessions in it, follow fitness over months and pick apart a single ride.

- Imports cover FIT, TCX, GPX, PWX, SRM, Polar HRM and many other formats, and some devices, such as SRM PowerControl units and PowerTap, download directly.
- Metrics include BikeStress, TRIMP and RPE, alongside models such as Critical Power, W'bal, Banister and the PMC.
- Aerolab estimates aerodynamic drag from your ride data.
- Train view runs indoor sessions with ANT+ and Bluetooth trainers.
- Strava, Withings, Dropbox and other services connect for upload and download.

When the built-in charts are not enough, you can write your own metrics and charts in its scripting language, in Python or in R. Those can be shared through GoldenCheetah's cloud. Through the OpenData initiative you can also give anonymized data to researchers.

Version 3.8 adds planned activities and runs natively on Apple silicon. The Apple silicon download needs macOS 15 or later, and the Intel one macOS 12.7 or later. Both are large, about 330 MB and 520 MB.
