WhichSpace puts the number of the active Space in the menu bar, so you always know which desktop you are on. It can also show every Space at once, including those on other displays, and clicking one switches to it. Each Space can have its own colors, icon shape, text label, font, symbol or emoji, and global shortcuts switch Spaces or send the front window to another one.

The app can be driven from Shortcuts, AppleScript and a `whichspace://` URL scheme, and it updates itself. It requires macOS 14 or later. Moving windows between Spaces works on Sonoma and on Tahoe 26.4 or later, but not on Sequoia.
