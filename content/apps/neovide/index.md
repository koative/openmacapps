Neovide puts Neovim in its own Mac window. Editing behaves the same as in the terminal, and your existing configuration and plugins keep working, but the window adds graphical touches a terminal cannot: smooth scrolling, an animated cursor, animated window movement, font ligatures and blurred floating windows.

It is a client only, so Neovim itself must be installed separately; current releases need Neovim 0.12 or newer on your `PATH`. Separate DMGs are published for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Neovide can also attach to a Neovim instance running on another machine.
