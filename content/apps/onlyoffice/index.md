ONLYOFFICE Desktop Editors opens Word, Excel and PowerPoint files from your Mac as tabs in one app and edits them without an internet connection. Office Open XML (.docx, .xlsx and .pptx) is its working format. It also converts formats such as ODT, RTF, EPUB, ODS, CSV and ODP.

- The PDF editor edits PDFs and fills in PDF forms, and the form creator builds new forms.
- Diagram files open in a viewer, without editing.
- Documents can carry digital signatures and password protection.
- Plugins and AI assistants add functions to the editors.

You can connect the app to a cloud such as ONLYOFFICE, Nextcloud, ownCloud, Seafile, Box or Dropbox and edit shared files together with other people. The interface comes in 46 languages and handles right-to-left text.

Microsoft Office uses these file formats natively and is sold as part of Microsoft 365. ONLYOFFICE Desktop Editors is free to download and licensed under the AGPL v3. The download page offers separate builds for Intel and Apple chips, both for macOS 10.13 or later, and an older build for macOS 10.12 and below.
