Tuta Mail for Mac is the desktop client for Tuta's encrypted email, calendar and contacts service. You sign in with a Tuta account, free or on a paid plan, and handle mail, events and contacts in one app.

Mailbox data is encrypted end to end, and the scheme is post-quantum. The company says it cannot read the contents. Calendar invitations can be sent encrypted to people on other providers, protected by a password you share with them.

Importing EML or MBOX files into Tuta is a feature of the desktop clients, and the free plan does not include it. That plan has 1 GB of storage and one calendar, while paid plans add custom domains and extra addresses.

The web and desktop clients and the Android and iOS apps are developed in one public repository under GPL-3.0. Desktop builds get their own release tags. Desktop release 366.261105.0, for example, carries a Mac disk image alongside a Windows installer and Linux AppImage.

The project closes issues and comments it suspects were written with the help of large language models, and asks for reports written by people, even incomplete or machine-translated ones.
