MeowOut puts several small utilities into one menu bar app: clipboard history with pinning, TOTP codes with secrets kept in the Keychain, a drag-and-drop dropzone for files, Markdown memos, a radial launcher, and live CPU, memory, network, battery and disk readings. A pixel cat, panda or pika walks in the menu bar while you work. It suits people who would rather have these tools in one menu bar app than several.

It needs macOS 14.0 or later. Builds are published for Apple silicon and Intel. The README calls the builds unsigned, so they are not notarized: remove the quarantine attribute from the DMG and allow the first launch in System Settings.
