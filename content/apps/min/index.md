Min keeps its own interface small so the page gets the room. Its tabs are slimmer, leaving more of the window to the page, tabs you have not looked at for a while fade out, and Focus Mode hides your other tabs while you work on one thing.

Tasks are Min's answer to a crowded tab bar. Each task is a group of tabs, and you switch between groups with keyboard shortcuts. A task you are not using collapses on its own.

- Search runs over the full text of pages you have visited, and that index stays on your device.
- Bookmarks take tags, and Min suggests pages similar to ones you have saved.
- Most ads and trackers are blocked without any setup.
- It includes an automatic reader view.
- It works with password managers and has a dark theme.

Min is written in JavaScript on Electron and released under the Apache 2.0 license. Each GitHub release carries separate ZIP downloads for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and the Apple silicon app for version 1.35.7 requires macOS 12 or later. Builds for Windows and Linux come from the same release.

The developers say Min does not share your browsing history. You can extend the browser with userscripts, and the project wiki documents how to write them and collects scripts from other users. The wiki also lists the keyboard shortcuts.
