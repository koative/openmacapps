Capture helper (native/akou-capture)
------------------------------------

hark (MIT), https://github.com/PhantomYdn/hark
  Copyright (c) 2026 Ilya Naryzhnyy.
  The capture design the helper ports to Rust: a private, unmuted Core Audio process tap read
  through a private aggregate device with tap auto-start and drift compensation, the throwaway
  probe tap that tells a dead tap from a quiet call, and the dead-tap rebuild rule. No hark code
  is copied.

Handy (MIT), https://github.com/cjpais/Handy
  Copyright (c) 2025 CJ Pais.
  The receipt rules of the dictation paste in native/akou-capture/src/dictate/insert.rs are
  ported from Handy's paste_tx: only reads after the paste chord count, the old clipboard comes
  back a quiet period after the last read or at a bounded timeout (a short one after a failed
  chord), and only while the clipboard is still ours. The MIT notice is in that file.

handy-keys (MIT), https://github.com/handy-computer/handy-keys
  Copyright (c) 2026 handy-computer.
  The macOS key tap of the dictation helper (native/akou-capture/src/dictate/mac.rs) is set up
  the way handy-keys' macOS listener is: an active session tap on its own run loop, enabled again
  from its own callback when macOS disables it, never polled. No handy-keys code is linked.

libopus (BSD-3-Clause), https://opus-codec.org
  Copyright (c) Xiph.Org Foundation, Skype Limited, Octasic, Jean-Marc Valin, Timothy B.
  Terriberry, CSIRO, Gregory Maxwell, Mark Borgerding, Erik de Castro Lopo and contributors.
  Built from source and linked statically through opusic-sys (BSD-3-Clause).

App (src/main)
--------------

FrequencyWords (content CC BY-SA 4.0, code MIT), https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
  Copyright (c) 2016 Hermit Dave.
  The word lists in src/main/vocab/dictionaries (en.txt.gz, es.txt.gz), which tell a real word
  from a mishearing when vocabulary corrections are applied, are adapted from its 2018 top-50,000
  frequency lists (built from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus, https://opus.nlpl.eu) at commit
  525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6: the counts are dropped and each word is lowercased,
  stripped of accents, split into words, deduplicated and sorted (scripts/build-dictionaries.ts).
  The content is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  licence, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/, which Creative Commons declared
  compatible with GPLv3 (https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/licensing-considerations/compatible-licenses/);
  the adapted lists are distributed under GPLv3 with the rest of akou.

double-metaphone 2.0.1 (MIT), https://github.com/words/double-metaphone
  Copyright (c) 2014 Titus Wormer.
  The English sound-alike key that scores a dictation fix as a learning candidate
  (src/core/dictation/learn.ts). Bundled into the app's main process from npm; its MIT notice is
  in the package.

transcribe-cpp 0.4.1 (MIT), https://github.com/handy-computer/transcribe.cpp
  Copyright (c) 2026 The transcribe.cpp authors.
  Runs Whisper large-v3 and Canary-1b-v2 in the final pass (src/main/asr/transcribe-cpp.ts). The
  npm package and its per-platform native package (@transcribe-cpp/<platform>) ship
  transcribe.cpp's library with ggml (MIT) and miniz (MIT); their licence texts are in the
  package's licenses folder. It loads them through koffi 3.3.2 (MIT), https://github.com/Koromix/koffi.

CrispASR v0.8.44 (MIT), https://github.com/CrispStrobe/CrispASR
  Runs the word-time aligner, Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B, on the best preset's words
  (src/main/asr/aligner.ts). Its release build is a download pinned by SHA-256, like the models
  (src/main/asr/aligner-catalog.ts); the -vulkan image carries the Linux build in /opt/crispasr with
  its LICENSE and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.
