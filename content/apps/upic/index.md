uPic uploads pictures and files from Finder, the clipboard, screenshots, drag and drop, the share sheet and the command line. It sends them to a configured host, including Amazon S3, Imgur, GitHub, Gitee, Aliyun OSS, Tencent COS and custom upload APIs, and copies the URL as plain text, HTML or Markdown. The menu bar shows upload progress and the history is kept in the app. It suits bloggers and anyone who often pastes images into chats and documents.

The App Store build is the current one. The README says GitHub downloads stay at v0.21.1, which is the zip linked here. The app needs Full Disk Access, which the README asks you to grant under Preferences, then Advanced.
