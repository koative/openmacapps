Browser Cookie Bridge copies signed-in sessions from Chromium browsers such as Chrome, Brave, Edge and Arc into ChatGPT Codex, and offers an experimental import into Cursor. Local transfers stay on the Mac, and the app has no account or analytics. It is for people who sign in to a site in a browser and want the same session in Codex without signing in again. Optional extras include an encrypted Grok Bot transfer file and an upload to Browserless Cloud.

The app requires macOS 13.5 or later and ships separate DMGs for Apple silicon and Intel. The Browserless upload runs only after you pick that destination and confirm it. Reading Brave cookies can require Full Disk Access for the app. The README says the current DMGs are Developer ID signed and Apple notarized.
