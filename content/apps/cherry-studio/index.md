Cherry Studio puts chats with several AI models in one window. You add providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini with your API keys, or point it at local models in Ollama or LM Studio. One question can then go to several models, with the answers compared in the same conversation.

- Agents get their own instructions, models and tool permissions, and can work with files, run commands and use MCP servers.
- Knowledge bases built from files, folders and web pages feed answers in chats and agents.
- Image models generate and edit pictures from prompts.
- Quick translation works from inside other apps, next to a Quick Assistant and text selection tools.

Responses render Markdown, code, formulas and Mermaid charts, and conversations can be exported. Dictation and spoken replies are available, including with local models. The project says conversation history and knowledge bases are kept on the device, with local and WebDAV backups.

What works depends on your provider and setup. Some features need API credentials, downloaded models, extra dependencies or system permissions. The GitHub release has separate DMGs for Apple silicon and Intel. The Community edition is licensed AGPL-3.0, and an Enterprise edition for teams adds central model management and access controls.
