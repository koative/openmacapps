Plug plays music from Hype Machine, a music blog aggregator, in a window of its own on the Mac. It requires a Hype Machine account. Its sections are Popular, Favorites, Latest, Blogs, Feed, Genres, Friends and Search.

From the app you can search for songs, mark them as favourites and follow artists. Popular has Now, Last Week, No Remixes and Only Remixes views, and Latest has All, Freshest, No Remixes and Only Remixes. Feed has All, Friends and Blogs. Favorites holds playlists named All, Up, Down and Weird, and search results sort by Newest, Most Favorites or Most Reblogged.

Settings has three on-off options: show notifications when changing tracks, hide tracks that are unavailable, and prevent sleep while music plays.

The current version, 3.6.0, is free on the Mac App Store and needs macOS 12.4 or later. For macOS 10.15, the README links an older 3.4.1 build as a ZIP. Localization is not planned.
