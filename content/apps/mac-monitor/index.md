Mac Monitor shows memory, CPU, network and disk figures in the menu bar, read locally from public macOS interfaces about once every one to three seconds. It is for people who want a small, dependency-free monitor that stays out of the way. The project's privacy section says the app makes no network requests and includes no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.

The release is a universal binary for Apple silicon and Intel and needs macOS 13 Ventura or later. The DMG is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so the first launch has to be allowed: on macOS 15 and later, use Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security; on macOS 13 and 14, right-click the app and choose Open.
