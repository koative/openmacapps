Ports is a menu bar app that lists the network ports open on your Mac, with the process name, protocol, and whether each port is local-only or exposed to the network. It also gives a risk indicator, lets you pin favourites, search and filter, and terminate a process from the menu. It is for developers who need to find out which process is holding a port.

It requires macOS 13 or later. The release zip is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. The project has one release, 0.1.0, from January 2026.
