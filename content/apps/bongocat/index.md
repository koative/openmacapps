BongoCat keeps an animated cat in a window on your desktop that reacts while you work. Its eyes and paws follow the pointer, and each press of a key, mouse button or gamepad button gets a matching motion. The window can be moved anywhere on the screen.

The cat is a Live2D model, and you can replace it. Load a model of your own, or download ones other people have shared through the Awesome-BongoCat list, where you can also post yours. The README credits Bongo-Cat-Mver by MMmmmoko as the inspiration.

BongoCat runs on macOS and Windows. Release 2.4.1 offers separate Mac disk images for Apple silicon and Intel, plus a Homebrew install. The release notes list macOS 12 as the minimum. The Apple silicon app in that release is not notarized.

The developer says BongoCat collects no user data and works offline without network access.
