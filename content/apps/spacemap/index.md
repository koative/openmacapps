SpaceMap draws the folders and files of a disk as nested rectangles, sized by how much space each one takes, so you can see at a glance what is filling it. Double-click a folder to zoom in, check the largest files in the sidebar, and move items to the Trash from the right-click menu. It suits people who want to find out what is using their storage before deleting anything. The interface is in English and Russian.

It requires macOS 13 or later and runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. The app is not signed with an Apple certificate and is not notarized, so the first launch has to be allowed in System Settings under Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway.
