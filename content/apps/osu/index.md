osu! is a rhythm game in which you hit circles, follow sliders and spin to the beat of songs, using a mouse, tablet, touch screen or keyboard. Besides the standard mode it includes taiko drumming, catch and a keyboard-based mania mode, and players can download a large library of community-made beatmaps or build their own in the editor.

This is "lazer", the open-source rewrite that is replacing the original Windows-only client. Online play, rankings and beatmap downloads need a free osu! account. Builds are provided for macOS 12 or later on Apple silicon and Intel.
