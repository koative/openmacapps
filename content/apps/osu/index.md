osu! is a rhythm game. In its main mode you hit circles, follow sliders and spin spinners in time with a song, using a mouse, a pen tablet, a touchscreen or the keyboard. Each song comes as a beatmap, and the beatmap library is made by the players.

Four modes ship with the game: osu! itself, taiko, catch and mania. Developers can add more as rulesets, which reuse the beatmap library and the game engine for a new style of play.

This client, codenamed lazer, is the open-source version the developers call the future and final iteration of osu!. The older client, osu!stable, is still offered for Windows. When you first start lazer, it asks you to sign in or create an account before you go looking for beatmaps.

Builds for macOS 12 or later come as separate zipped apps for Apple silicon and Intel. Release notes for each version are on the osu! website.
