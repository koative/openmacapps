FormRef collects images and files onto a freeform canvas where you can group them, add notes and draw connections. It can filter images by color or similarity and includes drawing grids and measurement tools. The downloadable Mac app runs locally and needs no account.

For a drawing study, use the value tool to reduce an image to distinct shades of gray or extract its palette. Comparison modes place two images side by side or use a swipe divider. FormRef imports BeeRef boards and can export a whole composition as SVG or PDF, or collect the original files in a ZIP.

FormRef puts analysis tools and media controls on the board. Linger keeps searchable notes and source URLs with images in a managed library. FormRef offers separate downloads for Apple silicon and Intel on macOS 12 or later.
