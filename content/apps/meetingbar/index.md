MeetingBar puts your current or next meeting in the menu bar and joins its call with one click. It reads the event, finds the meeting link and opens the matching call.

More than 50 services are recognized, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex, Slack Huddle, Discord and FaceTime among them. Events come from any account set up in Calendar, such as iCloud, Exchange or Google, or from Google Calendar connected directly.

- The menu bar can show the title, start time, a countdown or the service icon, with long titles shortened.
- A global keyboard shortcut joins the nearest meeting.
- Filters can drop all-day events, events without a link, and invitations you declined, marked tentative or left pending.
- Notifications warn you before a meeting, and a full-screen reminder can cover the screen for important ones.
- Shortcuts and AppleScript integrations are built in, and a custom AppleScript can run when you join, for example to pause music.

The developer says MeetingBar collects no personal data. It needs macOS 12 or later. The GitHub DMG holds a Universal app, signed with a Developer ID and notarized, and MeetingBar is also available from the Mac App Store. It is free under the Apache 2.0 license, with optional in-app purchases to support development.

You can also start an ad-hoc meeting in your preferred service, bookmark recurring meetings, and open events in Calendar or Fantastical. Each service can open in a chosen browser or in its own app.
