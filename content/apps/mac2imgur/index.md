mac2imgur watches for new macOS screenshots and uploads each one to Imgur. It then copies the direct link to the clipboard and sends a notification. You can also drop images on the status bar icon or choose Upload Images from its menu. Settings cover launch at login, deleting screenshots after upload, asking before each upload, and downscaling Retina captures. It suits people who often share screenshots as Imgur links.

The bundle is signed with a Developer ID and notarized, its minimum is macOS 10.9, and the executable is x86_64 only. The project is not affiliated with Imgur LLC, and its code is GPL-3.0 licensed.
