MenuHelper adds a Finder context menu of your own design. You choose the apps, and selected files or folders open in them directly, without going through Open With each time. The companion configuration app is SwiftUI. It suits people who regularly send files to the same few editors or viewers.

The README shows a macOS 14 compatibility badge and gives the GitHub release as a direct download, with the same app also on the Mac App Store.
