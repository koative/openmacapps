Itsypad combines a scratchpad for jotting text with a history of what you copied. Tap the left Option key three times, or set your own shortcut, and the app shows or hides. The clipboard history can have a separate hotkey.

The editor side works with tabs, which you can pin, reorder or split side by side or top to bottom. Tabs, their text and cursor positions come back after a restart, and content is saved as you type. Other editor features:

- Syntax highlighting for more than 185 languages through highlight.js, with the language detected automatically and nine themes in light and dark variants.
- Bullet, numbered and checkbox lists written as plain Markdown, so the text stays portable; Shift-Command-L turns lines into a checklist.
- A rendered Markdown preview next to `.md` tabs, updated while you type.
- Find and replace, clickable links and an option to keep the window above other apps.

The clipboard side keeps up to 1,000 text and image entries in a grid or as full-width rows. You can search them, move through them with the arrow keys and preview one with Space. Command-1 to 9 copies an entry, and Option-1 to 9 pastes it into the app you were using.

Through iCloud, scratch tabs and text clipboard entries can sync between Macs and the iPhone and iPad version. The developer says the app has no AI features and no telemetry. It can sit in the menu bar, the Dock or both, and the interface comes in 12 languages.

Itsypad needs macOS 14 or later. The GitHub DMG of version 1.18.0 holds a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, notarized by Apple. It is free under the MIT license; a paid App Store edition, bought once, also runs on iPhone and iPad.
