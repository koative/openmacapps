# Third-party content

## Word lists (`unfd/Resources/Words/ar.txt`, `unfd/Resources/Words/en.txt`)

Derived from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords),
files `content/2018/ar/ar_50k.txt` and `content/2018/en/en_50k.txt`, built from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus.
Changes: counts removed, words with anything but letters dropped, English lowercased; the order (most common first) is kept.
