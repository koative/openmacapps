IPTVMac plays live channels from IPTV providers that use Xtream Codes logins or M3U playlists. It has instant search across channels, a built-in player with subtitles and Picture in Picture, catch-up, and downloads for offline viewing. The app includes no channels of its own: you add your provider or a playlist. It suits people who already have an IPTV service and want a native Mac player.

It needs an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later; Intel Macs are not supported. The README says versions from 0.9.1 are signed with Developer ID and notarized, so the DMG opens with a double-click; older versions are not notarized and need Open Anyway in System Settings. There are no accounts or analytics, and the app talks only to the servers you add and to GitHub for updates. Many providers allow one connection per account, and downloads run one at a time.
