Keeper is a Mac app for going through a memory card or folder of photos with the keyboard. It reads each photo once, flags shots that look blurry, too dark, too bright or weak, and lets you delete the misses to the Trash, with their RAW files, and undo the last delete. Keepers can be added to the Photos library or to a new album. It is for photographers who need to sort a memory card quickly.

It requires macOS 15 or later. The release is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. The quality checks run on your Mac, and the README says their thresholds were set on a small sample of JPEGs, so they may need recalibrating for your own camera. Apple does not let apps create Shared Albums, so you must share an album from Photos yourself.
