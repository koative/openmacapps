Hacker Menu keeps the top stories from Hacker News in the menu bar, so headlines are one click away. It suits people who check the front page through the day and do not want a browser tab open for it.

The project is built with Electron. Its last release, 1.1.5, is from October 2015. The README gives no minimum macOS version and does not say whether the build is signed or notarized. Stories come from Hacker News' public API, which still answers; the app's update feed and former website are gone, so updates are manual.
