Time to Leave tells you when your working day is done. Punch in when you start, log lunch and other breaks, and it works out when you can go from the hours you set per day. When that time arrives, a notification tells you.

- Preferences set your working days, the daily hour target and whether notifications appear at all.
- A day view and a month view show what you logged, with your hour balance kept in sight above the punch button.
- Waivers mark days off, each with a reason such as vacation or sick leave.
- Holidays for your country, state and city can be pulled in as waivers.
- Light, Dark and Cadent Star themes come built in.

There is no database to configure. Since version 2.0.1 a day can hold several entries, and the app converted older data automatically. Version 4.0.0 dropped that converter, so if you still run 1.5.5 or earlier, install 3.0.0 first and then move to 4.0.0.

The app is built with Electron and has Windows and Linux versions too. Version 4.0.0, from August 2025, is the current release, and the Mac download is a disk image on GitHub. It added a Simplified Chinese translation to an interface already offered in many languages. The project's privacy policy says Time to Leave does not collect personal information.
