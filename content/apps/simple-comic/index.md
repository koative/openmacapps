Simple Comic opens a comic archive or a folder of images and shows it page by page, with as few clicks as possible between you and the next page. It reads CBZ, CBR, CB7 and CBT archives, plain ZIP, RAR, 7z, TAR and LHA files, PDFs, and JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP and HEIC images.

Pages can show one at a time or as two-page spreads. You can rotate them, zoom in and change the page order.

On Apple silicon Macs, Simple Comic runs Apple's text recognition on each page, so the words on a page become selectable. Drag across text to select it, Command-drag to add more, then copy it or have the Mac speak it from the Edit menu or a Control-click menu.

The bundled QuickComic extension adds Quick Look previews and Finder thumbnails for CBR and CBZ files. The developer says the app itself collects no user data.

This repository is a fork of the original Simple Comic by Dancing Tortoise Software, continued after its first maintainer went away. Version 1.9.9 needs macOS 11.5 or later. The GitHub ZIP holds a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, Developer ID-signed and notarized, and the same app is on the Mac App Store.
