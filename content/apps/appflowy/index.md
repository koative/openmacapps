AppFlowy keeps pages and databases in one workspace. You write documents built from blocks and store structured records in databases that you view as a grid, a board or a calendar.

- Grid views list records as rows, with filters that understand relative dates like today or tomorrow.
- Board views group cards into columns, with sorting and calculations per column.
- Calendar views place records by date, let events span several days, have a weekly view, and change dates when you drag an event.
- Relation properties link records across databases and can be used to group them.

The AI tools include search, a writing assistant, a chatbot and meeting notes with transcripts. The free Personal plan on AppFlowy Cloud limits several of them to a trial, and the pricing page lists a Local AI option.

The developers built AppFlowy as an answer to Notion, and both mix pages with databases. The difference is where it runs: the code is AGPL-3.0, and a team can host the server on its own machines, including air-gapped networks, in place of AppFlowy Cloud.

The desktop app is written in Flutter and Rust. Version 0.15.0 comes as Apple silicon, Intel and Universal disk images, and companion apps exist for iPhone and Android.
