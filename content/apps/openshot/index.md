OpenShot edits video on a timeline. Drag clips, images and audio onto as many tracks as you need, then trim, cut, scale and rotate them. Transitions preview in real time, and you can step through a clip frame by frame.

- Keyframe animation uses curves, and time-mapping plays clips faster, slower or backwards.
- Text comes from title templates, SVG titles, 3D animated titles and scrolling credits.
- Effects include brightness, gamma, hue, greyscale and chroma key, plus compositing with overlays and watermarks.
- Audio mixing and editing are built in.

Import and rendering go through FFmpeg, so common video, audio and image formats work, and timelines can be exchanged as EDL or XML. Version 4.1 added editing text directly on the video, automatic optimisation of footage at 4K and above, and a command-line interface.

The Mac download page offers a 64-bit Intel disk image, with daily builds listed below it for testing new changes.

Final Cut Pro, Apple's editor, is a paid App Store purchase or part of an Apple subscription, and it runs on Mac and iPad. OpenShot is free under the GPL, version 3 or later, and also runs on Linux and Windows.
