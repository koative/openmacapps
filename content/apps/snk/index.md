Snk puts the classic game of Snake in a Mac window. It comes from mowglii.com, the developer behind Itsycal. Press 1, 2 or 3 to choose a level and steer with the arrow keys or WASD. When the game ends, Space dismisses the game-over screen, or you type a level number to play again straight away.

Themes change how the board looks, and the window switches between a small and a large size. In version 1.2.1 the grid grew slightly to 15 by 14 squares, so that a game can actually be won by filling the whole board with snake. Music arrived in version 1.1.0, and the README credits three tracks: 8-Bit Melody by KingGJMaytryx, Bibo by Yo, L and Flight of the Bumblebee by 8BitUzz.

Snk 1.4.0 is a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel and requires macOS 11 or later. The ZIP on mowglii.com is Developer ID-signed and notarized, and the app updates itself through Sparkle. Version 1.1.3 stays available on the version history page for macOS 10.10 and later. The source is written in Swift and published under the MIT license.
