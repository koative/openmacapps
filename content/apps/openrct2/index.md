OpenRCT2 lets you build and run an amusement park in RollerCoaster Tycoon 2 on a reimplemented engine. You lay out rides, shops and facilities, then try to turn a profit and keep guests happy without losing the park's reputation.

Scenarios set an objective and a time limit. Sandbox mode is looser, and you can play it with no restrictions and no finances at all.

The project aims to cover everything the original game did and add to it:

- An improved interface and better guest and staff AI.
- More editing tools and higher limits.
- Cooperative multiplayer.
- Mechanics from the first RollerCoaster Tycoon that the sequel dropped, such as the in-game mountain tool and launched coasters.

The game needs the original RollerCoaster Tycoon 2 files, which you buy separately on Steam or GOG.com. Data from the first game with its expansions also works, which opens up the older scenarios.

The macOS download is a ZIP holding a Universal app. It runs on macOS 10.14 or later on Intel Macs and macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon. A separate launcher can keep the game updated for you.

Chris Sawyer wrote RollerCoaster Tycoon 2 in x86 assembly on an engine that grew out of Transport Tycoon. That older game has its own open-source counterpart, OpenTTD.
