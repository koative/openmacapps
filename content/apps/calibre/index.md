Calibre keeps your ebooks in one library and turns them into whatever format your reader needs. You add files, fix their details, then send them to a device by cable or wirelessly, and Calibre converts a book on the way when the device needs a different format.

- Metadata such as title, author, publisher, ISBN, tags, cover and summary can be downloaded from the internet or edited by hand, including custom fields.
- Three library views let you browse by cover, title, tag, author or publisher.
- The viewer opens all the major ebook formats, with highlights, bookmarks, a table of contents, read-aloud and search.
- A built-in editor works on the insides of EPUB and Kindle books.
- News recipes fetch articles from hundreds of sites or any RSS feed and package them as ebooks.

Conversion handles dozens of file types. While converting, it can restyle the text, build a table of contents and clean up punctuation and margins.

The Content server shares your library over the network. Anyone you allow can read books from it in a phone or tablet browser. A plugin browser inside the app installs community plugins in one click.

Calibre needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Older Macs have their own final versions on the download page: 7.26 for macOS 13, 6.29 for Big Sur and Monterey, and earlier releases for older systems. The app is free under the GPL-3.0 license.
