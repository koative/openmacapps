Joplin stores notes and to-dos as Markdown in notebooks that you search, tag and edit in the app or in your own text editor. Images and other attachments sit inside the notes.

Everything is kept on the Mac first, so notes stay readable without a connection. Sync is optional and works with Joplin Cloud, Nextcloud, Dropbox, OneDrive, S3, WebDAV or a folder on disk. Synced notes can be end-to-end encrypted. Joplin Cloud is the project's paid hosted service and adds sharing and publishing notes to the web.

- Evernote exports import with formatting, attachments and metadata such as creation dates and location.
- Plain Markdown files import as well.
- A Web Clipper for Firefox and Chrome saves pages and screenshots into a notebook.
- Plugins and themes change features and looks.
- The same notes open in the Windows, Linux, Android and iOS apps.

Joplin reads Evernote's export files directly, so a notebook moved from Evernote keeps its attachments and dates. After that, you choose where the notes sync, if anywhere.

The Mac release comes in separate builds for Apple silicon and Intel, as DMG, ZIP or PKG. The app is free under the AGPL v3 or later.
