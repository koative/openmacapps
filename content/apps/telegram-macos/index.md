Telegram for macOS puts your Telegram chats, groups and channels in a Mac window, separate from the cross-platform Telegram Desktop app that this catalog lists on its own. Your messages sync with your phone and other devices. The Mac app works on its own, so the phone does not need to stay online.

- Files of any type can be sent, up to 2 GB each.
- Groups can hold up to 200,000 members.
- Bots handle specific tasks inside chats.
- Voice messages and audio calls use the Mac's microphone, and calls can connect peer to peer.
- Secret Chats are end-to-end encrypted and can delete messages from both devices after a timer.
- Themes change how the app looks, and an editor works on photos and videos before you send them.

Regular chat history is stored in Telegram's cloud, so it takes no disk space on the Mac. Telegram says it never gives third parties access to your data or uses it for ads.

You can get the app from the Mac App Store, where it needs macOS 10.13 or later, or as a DMG from macos.telegram.org. A beta build is also offered for testing new features. The GitHub repository holds the source code under GPL v2. Translations and bug reports go through Telegram's own platforms rather than GitHub issues.
