This is the official source for Telegram's Mac-specific client, distinct from the cross-platform Telegram Desktop app listed separately in this catalog. It covers private and group chats, channels, voice and video calls, and media sharing with a macOS-style interface.

Telegram publishes it through the Mac App Store and as a direct download from its macOS site; the GitHub repository carries source but no binary releases. Using it requires a Telegram account and Telegram's servers.
