Slapss watches your calendar and, when a meeting is about to start, covers the screen with an alert and a button that opens the call. It recognizes Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Webex and similar links, and it shows the next meeting with a countdown in the menu bar. You can skip alerts for meetings such as lunch or ones you have not accepted, and it can show Reminders in the same full-screen form. Calendars are read on the Mac; Microsoft 365 uses a read-only sign-in kept in the keychain. It is for people who miss meetings because a normal notification goes unseen.

Requires macOS 15 or later. The README names the Mac App Store and the `theshiver` Homebrew cask as the only official builds, so this entry links the App Store, even though a ZIP is also attached to the GitHub releases. The app is free, with no subscription, in-app purchase, account, analytics or crash reporting.
