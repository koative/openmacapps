ScrollSnap captures content taller than the screen. Drag and resize its selection rectangle over a page or document, click Capture, scroll through the content, then click Save. The app finds where the frames overlap and joins them into one long image.

- Control-Option-S brings up the overlay from any app, and Settings can change or turn off that shortcut.
- Return or keypad Enter starts and stops a capture.
- The thumbnail of a result can be dragged into another app, swiped right to save, or right-clicked to show it in Finder or delete it.
- Captures go to the Desktop, Documents, Downloads, the clipboard or Preview.

The app's own overlay stays out of the image. ScrollSnap needs the Screen Recording permission, and its interface comes in nine languages, Romanian and Turkish among them. The 3.2.0 release on GitHub is a ZIP with a Universal app for macOS 14 or later, Developer ID-signed and notarized. New versions from GitHub have to be downloaded by hand, while the Mac App Store copy updates through the store. The code is MIT-licensed.

Version 3.2 improved stitching when you scroll fast, change direction or capture pages with sticky headers. It also warns when a capture may be incomplete and keeps what it got.
