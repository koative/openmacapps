Reminders MenuBar puts your Apple Reminders in a menu bar window. Click the icon, or press a global shortcut you set, to see what is due and tick items off without opening Reminders.

- New reminders can be typed in plain language, with the due date, list and tags picked out of the text.
- Due dates, priorities, recurrence, lists and tags can all be edited in place.
- An upcoming view shows what falls due within a time window you choose.
- Search runs across every reminder, and lists and tags work as filters.
- The menu bar item can show an icon, a count, or the next reminder due.

The app works on the system reminder store through Apple's EventKit, so these are the same reminders Apple Reminders shows, and they can sync through iCloud. On first use it asks for access to reminders, which you can manage later under Privacy & Security in System Settings.

Version 2.2.0 needs macOS 12 Monterey or later. It comes as a DMG or a ZIP holding a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, signed with a Developer ID and notarized. The interface comes in Turkish, Japanese, Ukrainian and more than a dozen other languages.
