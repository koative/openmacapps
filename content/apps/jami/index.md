Jami sends messages and places audio and video calls directly between devices, with no central server relaying the traffic. You create an account in the app without giving any personal information, and you can link several devices to that same account.

The macOS client is the Qt-based desktop app from Savoir-faire Linux. It requires macOS 13 or later on a 64-bit processor, and a separate beta build is offered alongside the stable one.

- One-to-one and group chats, plus recorded audio and video messages.
- Audio and video calls, video conferences and Rendezvous points that need no third-party host.
- Screen sharing and media streaming.
- An extension platform for add-on features, and a mode in which Jami acts as a SIP client.

Because connections run peer to peer, two devices on the same local network can still reach each other when the internet connection is down. The project says every conversation is end-to-end encrypted with perfect forward secrecy. Jami is a GNU project, with apps for Linux, Windows, Android, Android TV and iOS as well.
