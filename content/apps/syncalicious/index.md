Syncalicious keeps the macOS property list (preference) files you choose in sync between Macs, so a setting changed on one machine can reach another. It can also back those files up to a folder you pick, iCloud, Dropbox or Google Drive. It suits people who want their app preferences to follow them across Macs.

The README describes the project as in its early stages of development, shows a macOS 10.14 badge, and does not mention notarization. The latest release is 0.0.6, and the last commit is from December 2019.
