Streaker keeps your GitHub contribution streak in the menu bar. Its menu lists your best, current and previous streaks, along with your contributions today, your busiest day and the total for the year.

The menu bar icon tells you where you stand at a glance. It is empty when you have not contributed today, filled once you have, and wears a crown while you are on your best streak. You can choose between three icon themes, Flame, Tile and Octocat, each drawn for both light and dark menu bars. A reminder notification helps you keep the streak going.

Version 2.6.4 offers a disk image and a ZIP for Apple silicon Macs, with no Intel build, and the app inside declares macOS 13 as its minimum. You can also build it yourself with Node.js and pnpm.

Streaker is an Electron app and also runs on Windows and Linux. The same developer publishes Streaker CLI, a command-line version that draws an ASCII contribution graph, and Contribution, a parser that works out streaks from a GitHub contribution graph page.
