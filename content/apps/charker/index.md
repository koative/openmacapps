Charker shows the voltage, current and power of each port on an Anker Prime 160W (A2687) or 250W (A2345) charger, with a power timeline and energy use by day, week or month. It can also change some charger settings, such as port switches and charging modes. The 160W connects over Bluetooth, while the 250W signs in with your Anker account and reaches the charger over Wi-Fi through Anker's cloud. It suits owners who want to watch these chargers from the Mac.

Charker is an unofficial project and is not connected to or endorsed by Anker. It needs macOS 14 or later and runs on Apple silicon and Intel. The app is notarized by Apple. The README says only some controls were tested on hardware: the 160W screen settings and the 250W port switches, with the rest untested. Apple Home integration is off by default and needs iOS 27 to show power readings.
