mysa gives you a breathing break on demand. Press Control-Option-Command-B or click the gradient "m" in the menu bar, and the screen dims behind a frosted overlay while a small orb in the notch area breathes in and out to pace you. A developer joke draws itself across the screen in handwriting as you inhale and erases itself as you exhale.

While the overlay is up, mysa takes mouse and keyboard input away from other apps on purpose. Escape, the close button or the hotkey again ends the break.

- Seven breathing patterns are built in, among them 4-7-8, box breathing and pursed-lip breathing.
- Steppers in the notch let you build your own pattern from timed phases.
- A settings panel opened during a break changes tint, blur, grain and gradient while you watch.
- The hotkey can be changed from the icon's right-click menu, and it needs no Accessibility permission.

The single release, version 1.0 from June 2026, is a ZIP holding an Apple silicon app for macOS 14 or later. It is not notarized, so the first launch needs a right-click and Open. Building from source with the included script also registers mysa as a login item on first launch.

The author calls it a relaxation toy and says it is not a medical device.
