NotchPrompter puts your script at the top of the screen, in the notch area right next to the camera, so you can read it while you film yourself or talk on a call. It stays above other windows. You can also pin it to a corner or drag it anywhere as a floating window, and on a Mac without a notch it sits at the top edge.

- Voice activation listens through the microphone, scrolling the text while you talk and pausing when you stop.
- Speed, text size and font are adjustable, and the OpenDyslexic font is built in for easier reading.
- Global keyboard shortcuts start, stop and control the scrolling.
- With several monitors you choose the display the prompter appears on.

Version 2.7.0 added a timer, support for multiple scripts and an option to hide the Dock icon. The interface comes in several languages translated with DeepL, and the developer invites corrections.

The developer says the prompter does not show up in screen recordings or in screen sharing tools such as Zoom and Google Meet, and that the app collects no data.

The free download on GitHub, version 2.7.0, is a ZIP holding a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel that needs macOS 14.6 or later; it is not notarized. A newer build is sold once, without a subscription, on the Mac App Store, and buying it there funds the developer.
