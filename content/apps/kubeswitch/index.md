KubeSwitch is a small menu bar app that lists the Kubernetes cluster contexts available on your Mac, shows which one is current, and lets you change it. It suits people who work with several clusters and want to switch context from the menu bar.

The bundle declares macOS 10.13 or later. It is signed with a development certificate and is not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch until you allow it in System Settings. The project has had no commit since May 2019, and the download is a zip containing KubeSwitch.app.
