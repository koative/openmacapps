FileZilla Client moves files between your Mac and servers over FTP, FTPS and SFTP. Local and remote folders sit side by side, connections open in tabs, a site manager stores connection details, and a transfer queue handles large files, resumes interrupted transfers and respects speed limits. It can also compare directories, browse both sides in sync, search remote folders and edit remote files in place.

The macOS download from the project site is the plain app in a compressed archive, with separate builds for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. The same page offers paid editions: a version with a manual, and FileZilla Pro, which adds cloud storage such as S3, Google Drive and WebDAV; those protocols are not part of the free client. The site carries advertising and sponsor links.
