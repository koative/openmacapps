Clock.saver fills the screen with an analogue clock face modelled on a Braun wristwatch while your Mac is idle. You pick the watch model and its colour in the screen saver's options.

Four Braun ranges are drawn so far:

- BN0021 in black or brown
- BN0032 in white or black
- BN0095 in black or silver
- BN0111 Sports in blue, orange, grey, black or light grey

Unzip the download and double-click `Clock.saver` to install it. The `clocksaver` Homebrew cask does the same.

Version 0.7.3 from June 2023 is the latest release, and the repository has had no commits since. The bundle is Universal for Apple silicon and Intel, signed with the developer's Developer ID and notarized, and requires macOS 10.14.6 or later. Clock.saver is free under the MIT license.

The developer keeps a coverage list of the Braun models still missing and says he would like to add them all.
