title: Menu bar apps
description: Open-source Mac apps that live in the menu bar, such as small monitors, toggles, timers and quick panels you can reach from any screen.
tags:
  - menu-bar
---

Menu bar apps put a small icon at the top of the screen and open a menu or panel when you click it. They suit tools you check or toggle often, such as status readouts, switches, timers and quick capture, without adding a window or a Dock icon.

This list spans every category in the catalog, so browse by what you need rather than by name. A crowded menu bar gets hard to read, especially next to the notch on recent MacBooks, so it helps to choose apps that let you hide their icon or combine several functions in one menu.
