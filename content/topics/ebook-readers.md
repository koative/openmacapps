---
title: "Ebook readers"
description: "Open-source ebook readers for Mac that open EPUB, PDF and other formats, with libraries, highlights, notes and optional sync."
tags:
  - ebook-reader
---

An ebook reader opens book files such as EPUB, MOBI and PDF and presents them with adjustable fonts, layouts and themes. Most also keep a library of your books and let you highlight passages and take notes.

Check which formats each reader supports, whether it can sync reading progress and annotations between devices, and whether it works with DRM-free files only. Some apps add library management and format conversion, others focus on a single reading view.
