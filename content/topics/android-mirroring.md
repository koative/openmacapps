---
title: Android mirroring apps
description: Open-source Mac apps that mirror and control Android phones over USB or Wi-Fi, mostly as graphical front ends for scrcpy.
tags:
  - screen-mirroring
---

Mirroring an Android phone on a Mac puts its screen in a window that you can control with the keyboard and mouse. Most open-source options build on scrcpy, which streams the display over Android's debugging connection without installing an app on the phone.

The apps here wrap that in a graphical interface, with device lists, connection settings, recording and keyboard mapping, and one combines mirroring with emulators and simulators for app testing. Consider whether you connect over USB or Wi-Fi, how many devices you manage at once, and whether you need developer tools beyond mirroring.
