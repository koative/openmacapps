---
title: Photo managers
description: Open-source photo managers for Mac that catalogue large collections with albums, tags, metadata and search, without importing into a cloud service.
tags:
  - photo-library
---

A photo manager keeps a large collection browsable: it catalogues files by date, folder, place, camera or tags, reads and edits their metadata, and helps you find, rate and cull images. Unlike a cloud library, the apps here work with photos stored on your own drives.

They differ in emphasis. Some focus on organising and searching existing folders, including face and similarity search on the Mac, while another combines a library with a non-destructive RAW development workflow. Consider whether you want files left in their current folders, how much editing you need, and how the app stores its catalogue.
