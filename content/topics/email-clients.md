An email client downloads and sends mail for one or more accounts, usually over IMAP and SMTP, and keeps messages available on your Mac. Some of the clients below also manage calendars and contacts.

Check which providers and protocols each client supports, whether it offers a unified inbox, and how it handles encryption. Some apps work only with one provider's service, while others connect to any standard mail account.
