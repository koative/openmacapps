---
title: Time trackers
description: Open-source time trackers for Mac that log working hours or record which apps and sites you use, with the data kept on your own computer.
tags:
  - time-tracking
---

Time trackers answer where the day went. Some ask you to start and stop timers or log hours against projects, while others watch the active app, window or website in the background and build a timeline you can review later.

The apps here range from simple work-hour logs with exports for timesheets to automatic trackers and journals that summarise activity. Consider whether you want to record time by hand or have it captured for you, how you need to report it, and where the collected data is stored.
