title: Clipboard managers
description: Open-source clipboard managers for Mac that keep a searchable history of copied text, images and files, with keyboard shortcuts and local storage.
tags:

---

macOS remembers only the last thing you copied. A clipboard manager keeps a history of copied text, links, images and files so you can search it and paste an earlier item with a shortcut.

Every app below is open source, so you can check how your clipboard history is stored. Look for local-only storage, a way to exclude password managers and other sensitive apps, and a keyboard-driven picker that fits how you already work.
