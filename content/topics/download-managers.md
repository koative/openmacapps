---
title: Download managers
description: Open-source download managers for Mac that queue, resume and speed up downloads, with scheduling, browser integration and torrent support in some.
tags:
  - download-manager
---

Browsers download one file at a time with few controls. A download manager keeps a queue, splits files into several connections, resumes interrupted transfers and can limit speed or schedule work for later.

The apps here share those basics and differ in what else they accept: several handle BitTorrent and magnet links, and some fetch streaming media or offer browser extensions that hand links over automatically. Consider which link types you need, whether a native Mac interface matters to you, and how you want links to reach the app.
