Image editors cover several related jobs: retouching and compositing photos with layers and selections, developing RAW files from a camera, and painting or drawing with brushes. The apps below include examples of each.

Check whether you need layer-based editing, a non-destructive RAW workflow or painting tools, which file formats each app reads and writes, and whether it supports colour management and graphics tablets.
