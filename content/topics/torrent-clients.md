title: Torrent clients
description: Open-source BitTorrent apps for Mac that download and seed torrents and magnet links, from dedicated clients to download managers with torrent support.
tags:
  - bittorrent
---

A torrent client downloads files in pieces from other people sharing the same torrent and uploads pieces back while it runs. It handles torrent files and magnet links, and usually adds queues, bandwidth limits and per-file selection.

Some apps below are dedicated BitTorrent clients, including one that controls a client running on another machine and one that streams video while it downloads. Others are general download managers that also accept torrents alongside ordinary links. Consider whether you want a single-purpose client, remote control of a server, or one queue for every kind of download.
