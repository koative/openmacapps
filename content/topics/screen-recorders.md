---
title: Screen recorders
description: Open-source screen recorders for Mac that capture the screen, a window or an area with audio, from quick GIF clips to edited demo videos.
tags:
  - screen-recorder
---

macOS can record the screen through its screenshot toolbar, with few options for audio, formats or editing. Screen recorders add system audio and microphone capture, webcam overlays, output format and quality settings, and in some cases editors that add cursor zooms, captions and backgrounds.

The apps below range from small tools for short clips and GIFs to studios for streaming and polished product demos. Consider which audio sources you need to capture, which formats you deliver in, and whether recordings stay on your Mac or go to a sharing service.
