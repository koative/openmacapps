title: App launchers
description: Open-source app launchers for Mac that open apps, files and commands from a search box, a hotkey or a pop-up menu, as Spotlight alternatives.
tags:

category: Productivity
---

A launcher opens apps, files, web searches and other actions from a single entry point, usually a search box summoned with a hotkey. Many go beyond Spotlight with plugins, calculators, clipboard history, window controls or custom commands.

Some are built around typing a query, others around fixed key sequences or a menu that appears under the pointer. Consider how quickly results appear, whether the plugin system uses a language you know, and whether the app indexes files locally or sends queries to online services.
