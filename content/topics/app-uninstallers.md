---
title: App uninstallers
description: Open-source app uninstallers for Mac that find the preferences, caches and support files an app leaves behind and remove them with it.
tags:
  - uninstaller
---

Dragging an app to the Trash removes the app bundle but usually leaves its preferences, caches, containers and launch agents in your Library folder. An uninstaller looks for those related files and removes them together with the app.

Matching leftovers to an app is partly guesswork, so look for tools that show every file before deleting it and move items to the Trash rather than erasing them. Some of these apps are broader cleaners or Homebrew front ends with uninstalling as one feature among several.
