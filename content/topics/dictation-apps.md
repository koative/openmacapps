---
title: "Dictation apps"
description: "Open-source dictation apps for Mac: press a shortcut, speak, and get the transcribed text typed into any app, often fully on-device."
tags:
  - voice-typing
---

Dictation apps turn speech into text in whatever app you are using. You hold or press a shortcut, speak, and the transcript is pasted at the cursor. Many of the apps below run speech models such as Whisper or Parakeet on your Mac; others send audio to a cloud service.

Check where your audio is processed, which languages and models are supported, and whether the app rewrites or cleans up what you said. Push-to-talk versus toggle shortcuts, custom vocabulary and per-app settings also vary.
