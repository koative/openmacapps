---
title: VPN clients
description: Open-source VPN clients for Mac, from official apps for VPN services to clients for your own WireGuard or OpenVPN connections.
tags:
  - vpn-client
---

A VPN client routes your traffic through an encrypted tunnel to another network, either a commercial VPN service or a server you or your organisation runs. On a Mac it manages the connection, the tunnel configuration and when the tunnel turns on.

Some apps here are the official clients of a single service, others connect with WireGuard or OpenVPN configuration files you provide. An open-source client lets you see how the connection is handled on your Mac, but the privacy of a hosted service also depends on how that provider runs its servers.
