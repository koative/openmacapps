Loom records the screen together with a camera bubble and microphone and turns the result into a shareable link, so a walkthrough or update can replace a meeting. The open-source apps below cover that record-and-share job.

Cap records screen, camera and microphone, edits the clip and offers hosted sharing. Screendrop records quick video messages from the menu bar and can upload them to a Cloudflare account you control, so the videos stay on your own storage.
