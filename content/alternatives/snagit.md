Snagit captures screenshots and short screen recordings and opens them in an editor for arrows, text, callouts, blur and step numbers, mostly for documentation and support work. The open-source apps below share that capture-and-annotate job.

macshot, Snapzy, Shotnix, BetterShot and Screendrop each take area, window or full-screen captures, record the screen and provide markup tools. They differ in extras such as scrolling capture, OCR, redaction, upload targets and built-in video editing.
