Microsoft Outlook brings email from several accounts together with calendars and contacts in one app, and it is built around Microsoft's own Outlook.com, Exchange and Microsoft 365 accounts. The open-source mail clients below do the same core job on the Mac: they connect to the mail accounts you already have and give you one place to read, sort, search and send messages.

Thunderbird is the most complete counterpart, with mail, a calendar and an address book in one program, plus filters and add-ons. Betterbird is built on Thunderbird and layers its own fixes and usability changes on top. Mailspring concentrates on mail alone, with a unified inbox, snoozing, send later and templates. Support for work accounts on Exchange differs between the three, so check how your organisation's mail server is set up before moving over.
