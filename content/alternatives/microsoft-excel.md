Microsoft Excel organises data in workbooks of sheets, calculates with formulas and functions, and summarises the results in charts, tables and pivot tables. The open-source apps below cover the same core job of building and editing spreadsheets on the Mac, and both open and save .xlsx files.

LibreOffice Calc is the spreadsheet in the LibreOffice suite, with OpenDocument as its own format plus pivot tables, charts and macros. ONLYOFFICE Desktop Editors includes a spreadsheet editor built around the Office Open XML formats, with a ribbon-style toolbar similar to Excel's. Workbooks that rely on VBA macros, add-ins or recent Excel-only functions may not behave the same, so test those before switching.
