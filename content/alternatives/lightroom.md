Lightroom combines a photo catalogue with a RAW development workspace, where edits to exposure, colour, detail and lens corrections are stored as instructions instead of changing the original file. The open-source apps below do the same development job on your own RAW files.

darktable pairs a nondestructive editing pipeline with its own library and tagging. RawTherapee concentrates on detailed development and batch export without a full catalogue, and Redlamp is an early-stage developer whose panels follow Lightroom's Develop workspace closely.
