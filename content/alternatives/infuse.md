Infuse plays video files from local storage, network shares and media servers, and presents them as a library of films and TV shows with artwork and details. The open-source apps below share that library-first approach to watching your own video collection.

Kodi catalogues and plays films, shows, music and photos from local drives and network shares, with a ten-foot interface and add-ons. MyMedia is a native Mac app for movies and TV shows on your own disks, reading artwork and details from the metadata already embedded in each file.
