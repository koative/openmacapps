Typora edits Markdown documents with a live preview in the same view, so headings, lists and tables appear formatted while you write. The open-source editors below share the core job of writing and editing Markdown files on your Mac without a separate publishing step.

MarkText and Edmund work most like Typora, rendering formatting inline as you type. MacDown pairs the source with a side-by-side preview, and MarkEdit is a native source editor with Markdown syntax highlighting, which suits people who prefer to see the markup.
