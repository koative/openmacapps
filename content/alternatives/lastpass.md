LastPass stores passwords and secure notes in an encrypted online vault and fills them into websites through browser extensions. The open-source password managers below cover the same core job of keeping credentials in an encrypted vault, with source code you can inspect.

Bitwarden and Padloc work most like LastPass, with an account that syncs the vault through a hosted service or a server you run. Rowel stores its vault locally and can sync through cloud storage you choose. KeePassXC, MacPass, Strongbox and KeeWeb use KeePass database files that stay under your control, either on the Mac or in storage you already use.
