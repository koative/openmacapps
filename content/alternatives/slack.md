Slack is a messaging service for teams. Conversations are organised into channels and direct messages, with threads, file sharing, audio and video calls, and integrations with other work tools. Workspaces are hosted by Slack.

The open-source apps below are desktop clients for team chat platforms that do the same core job, and their servers can be self-hosted or used through a provider. Element connects to the decentralised Matrix network, Rocket.Chat offers channel-based chat with several workspaces in one window, and Zulip organises conversations into streams and named topics. Each needs an account on a matching server.
