Reeder is a reading app for Mac, iPhone and iPad that follows RSS feeds alongside other sources such as videos, podcasts and social posts. It presents them in a timeline whose position syncs through iCloud, and lets you save and tag links for later.

The open-source readers below do the same core job of subscribing to feeds and reading new articles on a Mac. NetNewsWire and Vienna are long-running Mac feed readers, Fluent Reader and RSS Guard offer several layouts and sync services, and Folo also follows newsletters, podcasts and social feeds. They differ in which sync services they support and how they handle full-text articles.
