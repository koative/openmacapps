Magnet arranges windows into tiles. Drag a window to an edge or corner, pick a position from the menu bar or press a shortcut, and it fills a half, third, quarter or the whole screen, across several displays.

The apps below do the same job of snapping individual windows into screen regions on demand, rather than tiling every window automatically. Rectangle and Nudge follow Magnet's model most closely, with edge snapping and shortcuts for fixed positions. Loop picks a position from a radial menu around the pointer, and MacsyZones lets you draw your own zone layouts and drop windows into them.
