PureRef collects reference images on one canvas, dropped from your files, pasted or dragged from a browser. Illustrators, painters and 3D artists use it to keep a moodboard or model sheet open while they work. It can stay on top of other windows, add quick notes, desaturate pictures and arrange and align them.

The open-source apps below do the same core job: they lay out reference images on a free canvas and save boards on your Mac. Some leave your files where they are, and others add study tools such as value studies and palettes. Check each one's notes on signing, since an app that is not notarized needs an extra step at first launch.
