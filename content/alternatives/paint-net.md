Paint.NET started as a replacement for the Paint app in Windows and became an image and photo editor with layers, unlimited undo, adjustments, effects and community plugins. It is made for Windows PCs only.

Seashore is the nearest match to Paint.NET for Mac: a native, layer-based editor that keeps the toolset small and approachable. GIMP covers the same ground with far more depth, including advanced selections, retouching tools and its own plugin ecosystem, at the cost of a busier interface.
