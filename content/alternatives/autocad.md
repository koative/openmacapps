AutoCAD is computer-aided design software best known for precise 2D drafting: floor plans, technical drawings and schematics built from layers, dimensions and exact geometry. The open-source apps below cover that 2D drafting job.

LibreCAD and QCAD Community Edition are both dedicated 2D CAD programs with layers, blocks, dimensions and snapping, and both work with DXF files. QCAD has a longer commercial history behind its community edition, while LibreCAD is fully community-run.
