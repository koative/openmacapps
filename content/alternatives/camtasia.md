Camtasia records the screen, camera and microphone and then edits the capture into a tutorial or walkthrough with cuts, zooms, annotations and captions. The open-source apps below share that record-then-edit workflow on the Mac.

Cap, OpenScreen, Recordly, Reframed and Screenize each pair a screen recorder with an editor for trimming and polishing demos, and most can generate zooms that follow the cursor. They differ in the extras: camera overlays, captions, iOS device capture or a multi-track timeline for fine-tuning.
