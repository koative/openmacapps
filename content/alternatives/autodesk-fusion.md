Autodesk Fusion, formerly Fusion 360, is parametric CAD software for designing mechanical parts from constrained sketches and features, with assemblies and manufacturing tools around it. The open-source apps below share the core job of parametric 3D part design.

FreeCAD is a large modular modeller with workbenches for parts, assemblies, technical drawings and more. SolveSpace is a compact program built around a constraint solver, suited to quick parametric parts and simple mechanisms.
