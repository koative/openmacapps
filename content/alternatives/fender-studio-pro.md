Fender Studio Pro, the workstation previously sold as Studio One Pro, is used to record instruments and vocals, program beats and parts, mix tracks and master the result. The open-source workstations below share that recording and production job on a Mac.

Ardour covers the recording side in depth, with multitrack tracking, non-destructive editing, MIDI, mixing and third-party plug-in support. Stargate bundles its own instruments, effects, pattern-based sequencer and wave editor into one app, trading plug-in hosting for a self-contained setup that runs well on modest hardware.
