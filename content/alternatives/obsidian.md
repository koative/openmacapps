Obsidian treats a folder of Markdown files as a personal knowledge base, connecting notes through wiki-style links and backlinks. The open-source apps below do the same core job of linking notes into a searchable web of ideas that you store yourself.

Tolaria and Knopo keep everything as plain Markdown files, Tolaria with Git-backed vaults and Knopo as a block-based outliner. Logseq is an outliner built around daily notes and linked references, SiYuan and Trilium Notes store notes in their own formats with block references or a note hierarchy, and Zettlr is a Markdown writing app with links and research tools for long-form work.
