Pixelmator Pro is a Mac image editor that handles layered photo editing, retouching, colour adjustments and simple graphic design in a single window. The open-source apps below share its core job of editing raster images in layers and exporting them in common formats.

GIMP offers the larger toolset, with advanced selections, filters, painting tools and scripting. Seashore is a lighter native Mac app for straightforward layer-based edits, closer in feel to a built-in Mac tool.
