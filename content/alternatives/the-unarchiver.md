The Unarchiver extracts compressed files that the built-in Archive Utility does not handle, such as RAR and 7z, along with a long list of older and less common formats.

The open-source apps below also open a wide range of archive formats on the Mac. PeaZip is a cross-platform archive manager that creates archives as well, with encryption, split volumes and integrity checks. MacPacker is a native app that shows an archive's contents like a folder, so you can preview files and drag out only the ones you need, and it can also create ZIP and 7z archives.
