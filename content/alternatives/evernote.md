Evernote collects notes, files and clipped web pages into notebooks, tags them and keeps everything searchable and synced across devices. The open-source apps below cover the same core job: a general notes library with notebooks or tags, search and synchronisation between computers and phones.

Joplin is the closest match in structure, with notebooks, to-dos, attachments and a choice of sync targets, including services you run yourself. Notesnook and Standard Notes put end-to-end encryption at the centre of their sync. All three publish their client source code, and their hosted services have their own plans and limits.
