Linger saves images, screenshots and source URLs to a local library. You can add notes to a saved reference, find related images by keywords or color, and arrange a selection on a canvas. It runs on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 14 or later.

FormRef supports local boards with notes, visual filters and study tools. ImageCanvas arranges images directly from local folders on boards saved on your Mac. Both let you keep references together while developing a visual idea.
