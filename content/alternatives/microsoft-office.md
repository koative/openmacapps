Microsoft Office, now sold as part of Microsoft 365, covers word processing, spreadsheets and presentations through Word, Excel and PowerPoint. The open-source suites below do the same core job on the Mac and open and save the Office file formats alongside their own.

LibreOffice is a full desktop suite built around the OpenDocument format, with separate apps for documents, spreadsheets, presentations, drawings and databases. ONLYOFFICE Desktop Editors uses a tabbed, ribbon-style toolbar and works with the Office Open XML formats natively. Complex documents can still look different after conversion, so check important files in either suite.
