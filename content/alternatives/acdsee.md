ACDSee Photo Studio for Mac is a photo management and editing app: it browses folders of images, organises them with keywords, ratings and categories, searches by metadata, and develops RAW files with nondestructive adjustments. The open-source apps below share that combined job of managing a photo collection and editing what is in it.

digiKam leans towards management, with albums, tags, face recognition, metadata editing and search for very large collections, plus an image editor and RAW import. darktable leans towards development, with a full nondestructive editing pipeline built on top of its own library, ratings and tags.
