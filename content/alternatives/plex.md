Plex organises a personal collection of films, TV shows and music with artwork and metadata, and plays it back from a server on your own hardware. The open-source apps below cover the Mac side of that job: browsing and playing your own media library.

Kodi is a self-contained media centre that catalogues and plays media from local drives and network shares, with a remote-friendly interface and add-ons. Jellyfin Desktop is the Mac client for Jellyfin, a separate open-source media server you install yourself, so it is the closer match if you want a server-and-client setup.
