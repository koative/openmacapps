µTorrent downloads files over BitTorrent from torrent files and magnet links, lets you limit upload and download speeds and manage several transfers at once, and in its Web edition plays media while it downloads. The open-source clients below do the same job on a Mac.

qBittorrent offers the fullest set of controls, including queues, priorities and per-torrent settings, while Transmission keeps to a smaller native Mac interface. Tribler can relay traffic through other Tribler users to hide who is downloading, at some cost in speed, and WebTorrent Desktop is built for streaming audio and video while a torrent downloads, connecting to both BitTorrent and WebTorrent peers.
